01 Start with your goal

Describe the result you want. Example: “Make this project update clearer for a nontechnical manager.” This is optional; it adds context, but does not choose commands for you.

Your goal adds context. You choose every command yourself.

02 Choose your commands

A command is a set of instructions for your AI tool. Click Add to select one. Start with one command; combine compatible commands when you need multiple steps. Search or browse by purpose to narrow the list.

Guided Questions and Quiz Me work on their own. Copy their prompt into your AI tool to start the conversation.

03 Add your material

Paste the text, proposal or topic you want the AI to work on. Example: a project update for /human, or study notes for Quiz Me. Evidence and review criteria appear only when relevant. A link alone is not verified evidence.

Your inputs stay in this browser tab. Reloading clears them.
